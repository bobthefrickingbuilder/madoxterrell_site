Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what personal information madoxterrell.com handles. The site is run by Madox Terrell, in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

The Short Version

This site doesn't use analytics or advertising, and doesn't set cookies of its own. The Spotify players on the home page are the exception: Spotify may use its own cookies. The site doesn't ask you to create an account, and it doesn't sell your information.

Information From Your Visit

Like any website, this one relies on a few outside services to reach your browser. They receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page:

  • Vercel hosts the site and may keep server logs of requests.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces.
  • unpkg supplies code libraries the site runs on.
  • Spotify provides the music players on the home page, and may set its own cookies and collect information about how you use them, especially if you're logged in to Spotify.

These companies handle that information under their own privacy policies, and may process it outside Canada. Madox doesn't use it to identify visitors.

If You Email or Use the Contact Form

If you write to madox.terrell@gmail.com or use the contact form, Madox receives your name and email address if you provide them, and whatever you include in your message. It's used only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and it isn't shared unless the law requires it or you ask.

The contact form is delivered by FormSubmit, an outside service that receives what you submit and forwards it by email under its own privacy terms. The email account is provided by Google, so messages are stored on its servers. Both may process information outside Canada.

Buying Scores and Other Sites

This site doesn't take payments. Remember is sold by Modern Sound Collective, on its own site. This site also links to Instagram, Facebook and the site of its web designer. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Your Choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information Madox holds about you, such as past emails, by writing to the address above. If you're not satisfied with the response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

This site isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has emailed personal information, write to the address above and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
