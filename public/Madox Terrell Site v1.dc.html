About

A multi-disciplinary musician with a background in composition for voice and more than a decade of performance experience, interested in choral composition, vocal performance and experimental sound design.

Madox Terrell is a composer, vocalist and producer based in Ottawa, Ontario. He holds a Bachelor of Music from the University of Ottawa (2024), with profiles in composition and voice. Since 2017 he has composed over three hours of choral music for commissions across Ontario, and has written and produced four albums under the name Madox, with over 100,000 streams across platforms.

From 2018 to 2024 he was Composer-in-Residence and Vocal Coach with the Ottawa Regional Youth Choir, writing over an hour of music for its seasonal concerts. He has sung as cantor since 2016, at St. John the Evangelist Church and now St. Joseph's Parish, and teaches piano, voice and digital audio production at Rockstar Music.
